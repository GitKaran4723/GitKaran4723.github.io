## 🧠 Vedic Mathematics: Fast Subtraction from Base Numbers

### 📌 Introduction
- This technique offers a **faster way to subtract numbers** from powers of 10 such as 100, 1000, 10000, etc.
- It replaces the traditional **borrowing method** with a much simpler and quicker Vedic Math approach.

---

### ❌ Traditional Subtraction Method
- Involves **borrowing digits** from left to right.
- Can be **time-consuming and confusing**, especially when dealing with large numbers.

---

### ✅ Vedic Math Trick: "All from 9 and the Last from 10"
- This powerful Vedic sutra helps perform subtraction quickly when subtracting from base numbers (like 100, 1000, 10000).

#### 💡 How It Works:
1. **Subtract every digit** of the number from **9**, except the last digit.
2. **Subtract the last digit** from **10**.

#### 🔢 Example:
To subtract **478 from 1000**:
- 9 – 4 = 5  
- 9 – 7 = 2  
- 10 – 8 = 2  
✅ Result: **522**
