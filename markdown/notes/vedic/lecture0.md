## 🧠 Introduction to Vedic Mathematics

### 📌 What is Vedic Mathematics?
- **Vedic Mathematics** is a collection of techniques (or *sutras*) derived from **ancient Indian texts called the Vedas**.
- It offers **simple and fast ways** to solve complex mathematical problems.
- It focuses mainly on mental math and pattern recognition.

2. 💪 **Confidence**: Improves mathematical thinking and confidence.

### 🔍 Examples of Vedic Maths Tricks

#### ✅ Example 1: **75 × 75**
📺 [Watch: (02:00)](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=be3fJeWFFpM&t=120)
1. Last digits: 5 × 5 = **25**
2. First digit (7) × next number (8): 7 × 8 = **56**
3. Combine: **5625**

#### ✅ Example 2: **98 × 97**
📺 [Watch: (02:22)](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=be3fJeWFFpM&t=142)
1. Nearest base: 100  
2. Differences:  
   - 98 – 100 = **–2**,  
   - 97 – 100 = **–3**
3. Multiply the differences: (–2) × (–3) = **06**
4. Subtract one difference from the other number: 98 – 3 = **95**
5. Combine: **9506**
