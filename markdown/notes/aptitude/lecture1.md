## ✅ **GENERAL STRATEGIES FOR SERIES QUESTIONS**

---

### 🔍 1. **Identify the Pattern**

The core step in solving any series question is **detecting the rule** or **logic** that connects the elements in the sequence. Patterns can be:

- Addition or Subtraction
- Multiplication or Division
- Powers (Squares/Cubes)
- Prime Numbers
- Fibonacci Series
- Alternate Terms
- Mixed Patterns

---

### 📌 2. **Look for Differences**

Start by checking the **difference between consecutive terms**.

#### 🔹 Examples:

**Series 1:** 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, ?

**Step:**  
8 - 4 = 4  
12 - 8 = 4  
16 - 12 = 4  
⇒ Constant difference of 4 (Arithmetic progression)

**Answer:** Next term = 20 + 4 = **24**

---

**Series 2:** 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, ?

**Step:**  
Check 1st level differences:  
4 - 1 = 3  
9 - 4 = 5  
16 - 9 = 7  
25 - 16 = 9  
⇒ Differences are increasing by 2

**Answer:** Next difference = 11 → 25 + 11 = **36**

---

### ✖️ 3. **Consider Multiplication or Division**

Check if each term is obtained by multiplying or dividing the previous term by a constant or a pattern.

#### 🔹 Examples:

**Series:** 3, 6, 12, 24, ?

**Step:**  
6 = 3 × 2  
12 = 6 × 2  
24 = 12 × 2  
⇒ Multiplied by 2

**Answer:** 24 × 2 = **48**

---

**Series:** 100, 50, 25, ?

**Step:**  
50 = 100 ÷ 2  
25 = 50 ÷ 2  
⇒ Divided by 2

**Answer:** 25 ÷ 2 = **12.5**

---

### 🧮 4. **Combined Operations (Multiply + Add/Subtract)**

Some series follow a **dual-operation rule**, such as:

**Series:** 2, 5, 11, 23, 47, ?

**Step:**
- 2 × 2 + 1 = 5  
- 5 × 2 + 1 = 11  
- 11 × 2 + 1 = 23  
- 23 × 2 + 1 = 47  
⇒ Multiply by 2, then add 1

**Answer:** 47 × 2 + 1 = **95**

---

### 🔢 5. **Recognize Prime Numbers**

Memorize the **prime numbers up to 100**:
2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, ...

#### 🔹 Examples:

**Series:** 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, ?

⇒ All are **prime numbers**

**Answer:** Next prime = **13**

---

### 🌀 6. **Look for Alternating Patterns**

Some sequences alternate between two patterns (like even and odd terms having different rules).

#### 🔹 Example:

**Series:** 2, 10, 3, 9, 4, 8, ?

- 1st, 3rd, 5th terms: 2, 3, 4 → +1
- 2nd, 4th, 6th terms: 10, 9, 8 → -1

**Answer:** Next term = 5 (continuing +1 series)

---

### 🔁 7. **Work Backwards**

Sometimes the pattern is **easier to detect from the end** of the series.

#### 🔹 Example:

**Series (ending):** ?, ?, 90, 60, 30

**Step:**  
30 → 60 → 90 → +30

Work backward:  
90 - 30 = 60  
60 - 30 = 30  
So the previous numbers are:

90 - 30 = 60  
60 - 30 = 30  
⇒ Before 90 = 120, then 150

**Answer:** Series = 150, 120, 90, 60, 30
