### ✅ **1. Seating Arrangement (Circular Seating)**

#### 📌 **Concept**:
Seating arrangement questions ask you to place people **in a circle** (or around a table) based on **directional and positional clues**.

---

#### 🔍 Important Assumptions:
- If **facing center**, then **left** is clockwise and **right** is counter-clockwise.
- If **facing outward**, directions **reverse**.

---

#### 🧠 Strategy:
1. Draw a circle with placeholders.
2. Read each statement and **mark direct positions** first.
3. Use **relative positioning** (e.g., “to the right of”) only after the fixed ones.
4. Keep checking for **consistency** with previous clues.

---

#### 📝 Example:
```text
Statements:
- A is to the immediate right of B
- C is opposite to A
- D is between B and C

Draw and test positions step by step until all fit.
```

---

### ✅ **2. Height Comparison (Order & Ranking Reasoning)**

#### 📌 **Concept**:
Determine the **order of height** among people using **comparative clues**.

---

#### 🧠 Strategy:
1. List all individuals.
2. Use symbols like `>` or `<` to denote taller/shorter relationships.
3. Merge the comparisons to form a **complete chain**.

---

#### 📝 Example:
```text
Statements:
- A is taller than B
- C is shorter than A
- B is taller than C

→ A > B > C
Answer: A is tallest, C is shortest
```

---

### ✅ **3. Row Arrangement (Linear Seating)**

#### 📌 **Concept**:
People are sitting in a **straight line**, and you’re given **left-right or position-based clues**.

---

#### 🔍 Assumptions:
- **Your left/right** is the same as the person’s **left/right** only if everyone is facing the same direction.
- Always check if the **direction is specified**.

---

#### 🧠 Strategy:
1. Draw blank boxes equal to the number of people.
2. Place people with **absolute clues** (e.g., “X is at the right end”).
3. Fill in the **relative clues** (“A is to the left of B”).
4. If facing **North**, left = left and right = right.
   If facing **South**, left/right are reversed.

---

### ✅ **4. Number Series**

#### 📌 **Concept**:
Identify patterns in a series of numbers to find the next number or missing number.

---

#### 🔍 Types of Patterns:

1. **Multiplication/Division Patterns**
   - Rapid increase/decrease.
   - E.g., `2, 4, 8, 16` → Multiply by 2

2. **Addition/Subtraction Patterns**
   - Gradual increase or decrease.
   - E.g., `5, 9, 13, 17` → Add 4

3. **Fibonacci-Type Sequences**
   - Each term is the sum of previous two.
   - E.g., `1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8`

4. **Alternating Series**
   - Different rules apply to odd/even positions.

5. **Combined Patterns**
   - Mix of +, ×, squares, or prime gaps.

---

#### 📝 Example:
```text
Series: 3, 6, 12, 24, ?

Pattern: Multiply by 2 → Answer = 48
```
