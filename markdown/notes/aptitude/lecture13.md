## 📘 **Detailed Notes: Percentage-Based Problem Solving**

---

### ✅ **1. Percentage Increase/Decrease between Two Values**

#### 📌 **Concept**:
When one value is a certain **percentage more or less** than another, the reverse comparison will **not have the same percentage value**.

---

### 🔷 **Case A: X is _x_% More Than Y**

#### 🔍 **To Find: How much is Y less than X (in %)?**

#### 🧠 **Assume Y = 100**

- X = Y + (x% of Y) = 100 + x

#### 📌 **Formula**:
```text
% Decrease of Y from X = x / (100 + x) × 100
```

#### 📝 Example:
**X is 25% more than Y**  
→ X = 125, Y = 100  
→ % Y is less than X = (25 / 125) × 100 = **20%**

---

### 🔷 **Case B: X is _x_% Less Than Y**

#### 🔍 **To Find: How much is Y more than X (in %)?**

#### 🧠 **Assume Y = 100**

- X = Y − (x% of Y) = 100 − x

#### 📌 **Formula**:
```text
% Increase of Y from X = x / (100 − x) × 100
```

#### 📝 Example:
**X is 50% less than Y**  
→ X = 50, Y = 100  
→ % Y is more than X = (50 / 50) × 100 = **100%**

---

### ✅ **2. Expenditure and Consumption Relationship**

#### 📌 **Concept**:
If the **price of a commodity increases**, and a person wants to keep the **total expenditure same**, they must reduce **consumption** proportionally.

---

#### 🧠 Relationship:
```text
Expenditure = Price × Quantity (Consumption)
```

To **keep expenditure constant**, an **increase in price** must be matched by a **decrease in quantity**.

---

### 🔷 **Example: Price of Petrol Increases by 50%**

#### 🔍 Question:
By what percent should consumption be reduced to keep expenditure the same?

#### 🧠 Assume:
- Original price = ₹100
- New price = ₹150
- To keep expenditure same, adjust consumption accordingly

---

#### 📌 **Shortcut Formula** (when price increases by x%):
```text
% Reduction in consumption = x / (100 + x) × 100
```

#### 📝 Applying:
Price ↑ by 50%, so:
→ % Reduction = (50 / 150) × 100 = **33.33%**

---

### 🔷 **What if Price Decreases?**

If the **price decreases**, and expenditure is to be **kept the same**, then **consumption increases**.

#### 📌 Formula:
```text
% Increase in consumption = x / (100 − x) × 100
```

#### 📝 Example:
Price drops by 20%  
→ % Increase in quantity = 20 / (100 − 20) × 100 = 20 / 80 × 100 = **25%**

---

## 🧠 **Summary of Key Shortcut Formulas**

| Scenario                                    | Formula                                     |
|--------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| X is x% more than Y                         | Y is less than X by: x / (100 + x) × 100    |
| X is x% less than Y                         | Y is more than X by: x / (100 − x) × 100    |
| Price increases x% → Reduce consumption     | x / (100 + x) × 100                          |
| Price decreases x% → Increase consumption   | x / (100 − x) × 100                          |

---

## 💡 **Tips for Quick Solving**

- Always **assume base = 100** to simplify.
- Use **shortcut formulas** to save time in exams.
- Carefully read whether you are comparing **more** or **less**.
- Practice using these formulas with different values to gain speed.
