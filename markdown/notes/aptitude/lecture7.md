### ✅ **1. Identifying Similarities (Odd One In or Out Based on Properties)**

#### 📌 **Concept**:  
You’re given a list of objects. The task is to **identify a common characteristic** shared by some and pick the one that **best fits or stands out** based on logic.

#### 🔍 How to Approach:
- Identify physical or chemical properties (e.g., metal, non-metal, solid, liquid).
- Look for **category or property matching** (e.g., metal + solid).
  
#### 📝 Example:
```text
Options: Mercury, Iron, Oxygen, Copper

- Mercury: Metal but liquid at room temp  
- Iron: Metal and solid  
- Oxygen: Non-metal and gas  
- Copper: Metal and solid

Correct Answer: Iron or Copper  
Reason: Only these are **metal + solid**
```

#### 💡 Tip:
- Always check **state, type, and behavior** of the elements/objects.

### ✅ **2. Finding Relationships Among Three Items**

#### 📌 **Concept**:  
You're given **three words or objects**. You must identify the **relationship or hierarchy** among them (like part–whole, function–tool, category–member).

#### 📝 Example:
```text
Options: Dog, Animal, Cat

Relationship: Dog and Cat are types of Animals → Category–Member
```

### ✅ **3. Alphabet Analogy**

#### 📌 **Concept**:  
These questions involve **finding a pattern between two sets of letters** and applying the same logic to determine the missing pair.

---

#### 🔍 Common Techniques:

1. **Alphabet Positioning**:  
   - A = 1, B = 2, ..., Z = 26
2. **Positional Shifts**:  
   - Letter moved forward or backward by a fixed count.
3. **Mirror Letters**:  
   - A ↔ Z, B ↔ Y, etc.
4. **Pairs Relationship**:  
   - First pair: A → C (+2), apply same to second set.

---

#### 📝 Example:
```text
Q: AB : CD :: EF : ?

AB → CD → A(+2)=C, B(+2)=D  
EF → Apply same: E(+2)=G, F(+2)=H  
Answer: GH
```

---

### ✅ **4. Similar Letter Series (Letter–Number Relationship)**

#### 📌 **Concept**:  
The question gives a series of letters where each corresponds to a specific number or follows a numerical pattern based on its **alphabetical position**.

---

#### 🔍 Example Pattern:
```text
Series: A (1), D (4), G (7), J (10)  
Pattern: +3 to each letter’s position → Next = M (13)
```

---

### ✅ **5. Number-Based Analogy Questions**

#### 📌 **Concept**:  
You’re given two related numbers and are asked to find the **same relationship** between another set.

---

#### 🔍 Common Number Relationships:
- Addition/Subtraction  
- Multiplication/Division  
- Squaring/Cubing  
- Concatenation  
- Digit-sum/digit-based logic

---

#### 📝 Example:
```text
Q: 2 : 4 :: 3 : ?

Logic: 2 × 2 = 4  
→ 3 × 2 = 6  
Answer: 6
```

#### 📝 Advanced Example:
```text
Q: 12 : 21 :: 23 : ?

Logic: Reverse the digits  
→ 12 → 21  
→ 23 → 32  
Answer: 32
```

### ✅ **6. Tips for Efficient Problem Solving**

#### ⏱️ Speed Strategies:
- **Write down letter positions** (A=1 to Z=26) for quick reference.
- **Eliminate impossible options first**.
- Look for **simplest arithmetic operations** first (before trying complex patterns).
- In analogies, **solve first pair completely** before even looking at options.
