## 🧠 **Verbal Reasoning – Detailed Notes**

---

### ✅ **1. Odd Man Out**

#### 📌 **Concept**:  
You are given a group of words or items, and you must select the one that **doesn't fit** with the others based on category, meaning, function, or characteristics.

#### 🔍 **Types of Differences**:
- Belonging to a different **category** (e.g., bird vs. mammal)
- Different **dwelling/place**
- **Function/use**
- Based on **meaning or association**

#### 📝 Example:
```text
Peacock, Sparrow, Vulture, Swan

Odd One: Swan  
Reason: Swan is an aquatic bird, others are terrestrial.
```

---

### ✅ **2. Bird Dwelling (Habitat-Based Odd One Out)**

#### 📌 **Concept**:  
Some birds have unique **natural habitats** that distinguish them from others.

#### 📝 Example:
```text
Peacock – Forest  
Sparrow – Urban/Trees  
Vulture – Mountains/Plains  
Swan – Water (Lakes, Ponds)

Odd One: Swan (Only aquatic)
```

---

### ✅ **3. Word Knowledge (Vocabulary-Based Reasoning)**

#### 📌 **Concept**:  
This tests your **understanding of collective nouns**, definitions, and group terminology.

#### 🔍 **Key Collective Nouns**:
- Herd → Group of **cows**
- Flock → Group of **sheep/birds**
- Shoal → Group of **fish**
- Crowd → Group of **people** (generic term, often used loosely)

#### 📝 Example:
```text
Herd, Flock, Shoal, Crowd  
Odd One: Crowd (Others are animal-specific terms)
```

---

### ✅ **4. Finding Relationships (Pair Matching)**

#### 📌 **Concept**:  
You are given multiple **pairs of related words**. One of them **doesn’t follow** the same kind of relationship as the others.

#### 📝 Example:
```text
Energy – Joule  
Current – Ampere  
Pressure – Pascal  
Angle – Degree

Odd One: Angle – Degree  
Why? Because the others are physical quantities and their **SI units**, whereas angle–degree is **not an SI unit** (SI unit of angle is radian).
```

---

### ✅ **5. Multiple Correct Answers**

#### 📌 **Concept**:  
Some tricky questions may have **more than one option that seems logically correct**, but you must select the **most appropriate or specific** one based on context.

#### 🧠 **Tip**:  
In such cases:
- Use **elimination technique**.
- Prioritize **specific technical correctness** over general association.
- Follow exam-setter’s intent.

---

### ✅ **6. Word Association**

#### 📌 **Concept**:  
You must understand **how two words are related**—often through **origin, function, or composition**.

#### 📝 Examples:
1. **Apparel – Clothes**  
   - Apparel = things we wear  
   - Clothes = the material used to make them  
   - Relationship: **End product made from base material**

2. **Footwear – Leather**  
   - Footwear is made from leather  
   - Same logic applies

---

### ✅ **7. Analogies (Verbal Reasoning)**

#### 📌 **Concept**:  
Analogies ask you to find a **relationship between two words** and apply the same pattern to a second pair.

#### 📝 Example:
```text
Delicious : Taste :: Melodious : ?

Answer: Voice  
Reason: Delicious describes taste; Melodious describes voice.
```

#### 🔍 Common Analogy Relationships:
- Cause–Effect
- Function
- Degree/Intensity
- Synonym/Antonym
- Part–Whole

---

### ✅ **8. Units of Measurement**

#### 📌 **Concept**:  
This topic involves identifying the **correct units** for physical quantities.

#### 🔍 Examples:
- Weight → Kilogram (kg)
- Energy → Joule (J)
- Force → Newton (N)
- Current → Ampere (A)
- Temperature → Kelvin (K)
- Pressure → Pascal (Pa)

#### 📝 Example:
```text
Q: Which of the following is not matched correctly?
1. Length – Meter  
2. Mass – Kilogram  
3. Pressure – Degree  
4. Energy – Joule

Answer: 3 (Pressure → Pascal, not Degree)
```

---

## 🌟 **Tips to Solve These Questions**

- ✅ **Build your vocabulary**—learn collective nouns, word origins, and common pairings.
- ✅ **Understand SI units** and their correct associations with physical quantities.
- ✅ **Compare options systematically**—eliminate those with clear category mismatches.
- ✅ **Practice analogies** by analyzing the relationship deeply before matching.
