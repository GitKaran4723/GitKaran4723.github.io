## 📊 **Percentage – Detailed Notes**

---

### ✅ **1. Basics of Percentage**

#### 📌 **Concept**:
- The term **"percent"** means **"per 100"**.
- It expresses a number **as a part of 100**.

#### 🧠 Key Idea:
- **100%** represents the **whole** (full quantity).
- **50%** means **half** the quantity.
- **0%** means **nothing** of the quantity.

#### 📝 Examples:
- 1 = 100%  
- 0.5 = 50%  
- 0.25 = 25%

---

### ✅ **2. Converting Numbers to Percentages**

#### 📌 **Formula**:
```text
Number × 100 = Percentage
```

#### 📝 Examples:
- 1 → 1 × 100 = **100%**  
- 3 → 3 × 100 = **300%**

This helps in comparing values that are more than 1 (e.g., profit = 150%, population = 120%, etc.)

---

### ✅ **3. Converting Percentages to Numbers**

#### 📌 **Formula**:
```text
Percentage × (1/100) = Decimal/Number
```

#### 📝 Examples:
- 100% → 100 × 1/100 = **1**  
- 25% → 25 × 1/100 = **0.25**  
- 10% of 200 → (10/100) × 200 = **20**

---

### ✅ **4. Converting Fractions to Percentages**

#### 📌 **Formula**:
```text
Fraction × 100 = Percentage
```

#### 📝 Examples:
- 1/2 × 100 = 50%  
- 3/4 × 100 = 75%  
- 2/5 × 100 = 40%

---

---

### ✅ **6. Converting Percentage to Fraction**

#### 📌 **Formula**:
```text
Percentage ÷ 100 = Fraction
```

#### 📝 Examples:
- 12% = 12/100 = **3/25**  
- 75% = 75/100 = **3/4**

This is useful in profit/loss, discount, and ratio-based percentage problems.

---

### ✅ **7. Finding Percentage of a Number**

#### 📌 **Formula**:
```text
(Part ÷ Whole) × 100 = Percentage
```

#### 📝 Examples:
- What % is 36 of 100?  
  → (36/100) × 100 = **36%**

- What % is 25 of 50?  
  → (25/50) × 100 = **50%**

---

### ✅ **8. Proportion Concept in Percentages**

#### 📌 **Concept**:
Use **ratios** or **unitary method** to simplify percentage calculations.

#### 📝 Example:
- If 20% of a number is 30, find the number.  
  → 20% = 30  
  → (20/100) × X = 30  
  → X = (30 × 100)/20 = **150**

This method is especially useful in percentage increase/decrease questions.

---

### ✅ **9. Solving Word Problems Involving Percentages**

#### 📌 Steps to Solve:
1. Identify what is being asked (Increase, decrease, comparison, etc.)
2. Write the correct mathematical expression.
3. Apply known formulas:
   - Increase = Original × (1 + rate/100)
   - Decrease = Original × (1 – rate/100)

#### 📝 Example:
**Q**: A price increases by 20%. Original price is ₹500. What is the new price?

→ New price = 500 × (1 + 20/100) = 500 × 1.2 = ₹**600**
