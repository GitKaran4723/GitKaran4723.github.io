### 🧠 **What is Average?**
- **Definition**:  
  Average = (Sum of observations) ÷ (Number of observations)

- **Purpose**:  
  - Represents a **single value** summarizing a dataset.
  - Helps to understand data at a glance.

---

### ✨ **Tricks & Key Ideas**

#### 1. **Average of Consecutive Numbers**
- If numbers are **odd in count** → Average = **Middle number**  
  (e.g., Average of 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 = 3)

- If numbers are **even in count** → Average = **Mean of two middle numbers**  
  (e.g., Average of 2, 4, 6, 8 = (4 + 6)/2 = 5)

---

#### 2. **Effect of Adding/Subtracting a Constant (K)**
- If **K is added** to every number → Average also increases by **K**
- If **K is subtracted** from every number → Average decreases by **K**

✅ *Shortcut*:  
Original average = A  
New average = A ± K

---

#### 3. **Effect of Multiplying/Dividing by a Constant (K)**
- If each number is **multiplied by K** → Average also gets **multiplied by K**
- If each number is **divided by K** → Average also gets **divided by K**

✅ *Shortcut*:  
Original average = A  
New average = A × K (or A ÷ K)

---

### 🧪 **Example Ideas (Practice Concepts)**

1. **Basic Calculation**:  
   Find the average of 5, 10, 15 → (5 + 10 + 15)/3 = 10

2. **Change in Average**:  
   Original average of marks is 70. If 5 is added to every mark, new average = 75

3. **Consecutive Number Sets**:  
   Average of first 10 natural numbers = (1 + 10)/2 = 5.5
