# Historical Background

The British came to India in **1608** as traders.  
They landed at _Surat_ on the western coast.

Gradually, they expanded political influence through:

- Military power  
- Diplomacy  
- Exploiting divisions among Indian rulers

---

## Important Treaties

**Treaty of Allahabad (1765)**: Recognized British revenue rights in Bengal.  
**Subsidiary Alliance** _(Lord Wellesley)_: Forced Indian states to accept British troops.
